# Natural Earth Land Data

`ne_50m_land.geojson` is Natural Earth v5.1.2's 1:50m physical land dataset.
It is bundled for offline, reproducible land filtering and coastline clipping.

Source: https://github.com/nvkelso/natural-earth-vector/tree/v5.1.2
